about

I study mathematics at the University of Waterloo and spend most of my time shipping software: planners, chat clients, and research tooling. I like problems where the fix has to be felt, not just described: latency, drag-and-drop, the exact moment a UI responds.

My most recent role was Software Engineer Intern at Unmodal Research Inc. (Jan 2026 – Sep 2026). Shipped responsive production features with Next.js, React, TypeScript, NestJS, and PostgreSQL, reducing UI duplication across frontend and backend systems.

Away from the editor I'm usually deep in a keyboard build: lubing switches, tuning a layout, chasing a sound. It's the same fixation on how a thing feels that I bring to everything else.
